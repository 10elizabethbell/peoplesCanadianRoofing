# PRODUCT — People's Canadian Roofing

- **Platform:** web
- **Stack:** static single-file HTML/CSS/JS (`index.html`), no frameworks, no tracking, system font stack. Hosted on GitHub Pages.
- **Surface mode:** Persuade (one-page site for a local trade business)

## Users
- Homeowners in Windsor-Essex County and Chatham-Kent, Ontario, with a roof that leaks or is due for replacement. They usually arrive from a Facebook post or group (Essex County Contractor Referrals) on a phone, inside Facebook's in-app browser. *(inferred from how the business advertises)*
- The owner, first: the site is given to the business free. It has to read as their own real website within one phone screen.

## Purpose
Turn a caption-less Facebook flyer into a place a homeowner can land, trust, and ask for a free estimate from, including after hours.

## Positioning (from the business's own flyer, 2026-10-19)
- Name as printed: PEOPLE'S CANADIAN ROOFING
- "Roof Replacements" · "Roof Repairs & Leak Fixes"
- "Licensed & Insured" (printed on the flyer; not independently verified)
- "Serving Windsor-Essex County & Chatham-Kent"
- "LOCALLY OWNED IN WINDSOR, ONTARIO"
- "Call Us For a FREE ESTIMATE! 519-999-2445"

## Capabilities and constraints
- Contact: phone 519-999-2445 (verified on flyer image). Whether it accepts texts is unknown; calling is the business's stated preference.
- No email, no business Facebook page, no Maps listing, no reviews, no work photos, no logo file, no hours, no prices, no years in business.
- Owner: a Mike Peoples posted the ad from a personal profile; his role in the business is unconfirmed, so he is not named on the page.

## Brand commitments
- Colors from the flyer: Canadian flag red, white, flyer gold/yellow, deep navy. Maple leaf as the mark. No black page ground.
- Voice: straightforward, "we" (roofing is crew work; crew size is unknown — inferred).

## Evidence on hand
- One flyer image (Facebook group post). No photos of finished roofs.

## Principles
- Only facts from the flyer. Nothing invented: no prices, reviews, years, warranties, brands.
- Phone first. The first phone screen says who, what, where, and how to get a free estimate.
- The free estimate is the main action; calling stays one tap away in the header.
